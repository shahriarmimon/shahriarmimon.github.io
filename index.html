Core Research Architecture

Operating at the convergence of computational structural biophysics, biomolecular condensation (LLPS), and quantitative crop genetics to accelerate sustainable climate adaptation.

Pillar 01

Computational Biophysics & Stress LLPS

Screened 118,230 transcripts across Arabidopsis, Oryza sativa, and Glycine max. Engineered a 3-tier framework coupling SaProt 650M structure-aware language models with a 5-axis thermodynamic stress engine (Heat/LCST, Drought/m6A, Cold/UCST, pH, and Salinity).

  • Resolved AlphaFold Structural Paradox using PAE & pLDDT sticker-aware filtering (100% specificity).
  • Identified 23 ancestral triplet hubs conserved over 150 million years across dicots and monocots.
  • Deciphered lineage-specific whole-genome duplication divergence in soybean paralogs lowering Csat.
Pillar 02

Quantitative Breeding & Explainable AI

Multi-generational field phenomics tracking 463 pedigree plants across 5 generations (P → F4) at SAU. Quantified spectrophotometric lycopene, titrimetric Vitamin C, soluble solids (°Bx), and ambient postharvest decay kinetics without cold storage.

  • Machine learning (Random Forest, R2 = 0.753 for lycopene) with microclimate enrichment (+13.4% accuracy).
  • Tree SHAP uncovered an empirical 31.1g fruit dilution tipping point decoupling mass from sugar dilution.
  • Identified Pareto-optimal transgressive segregants (F4-05, F4-12, F4-16) achieving up to 25.0 days shelf life.
Pillar 03

Phytochemistry & Agroecosystem Safety

Investigated defensive secondary plant metabolites for bioactive targeting via molecular docking, dynamic simulations (MD), and MM-GBSA binding free energies; synthesized rhizosphere microplastic contamination hazards in food systems.

  • Co-authored Adenium obesum kinase inhibitor discovery published in PLoS ONE (2026).
  • Lead-authored Brassica juncea dual-target PARP1/CK2α evaluation in ChemistrySelect (Under Review).
  • Published empirical review on microplastics in agricultural soils and root-zone translocation in Journal Clean WAS.
