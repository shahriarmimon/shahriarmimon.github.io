Active Projects

Plant Stress Granules & Biomolecular Condensates (LLPS)

Ongoing
Computational Plant Biophysics · IUBAT

Computational modeling of stress-responsive RNA-binding proteins across Arabidopsis thaliana, rice (Oryza sativa), and soybean (Glycine max). Investigating how environmental shocks (heat, drought, cold, salinity, and cytoplasmic acidification) trigger liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS) to assemble stress granules as rapid cellular defense switches.

Identification of Plant Secondary Metabolites for EGFR Inhibition in Glioblastoma (GBM)

Ongoing
Computational Structural Biology & Drug Discovery

Screening plant-derived bioactive secondary metabolites to identify novel epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) kinase inhibitors for glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) using molecular docking and molecular dynamics (MD) simulations.

Previous Projects

Tomato Pedigree Phenomics & Postharvest Durability

2026
Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU)

Evaluated 463 pedigree observations across five filial cohorts (P → F4) in tomato (Solanum lycopersicum). Conducted laboratory assays for lycopene, vitamin C, total soluble solids (°Brix), and ambient postharvest decay kinetics alongside machine learning analysis to identify resilient, high-quality breeding lines.

2025 - 2026
IUBAT

Computational evaluation of secondary metabolites derived from Indian mustard (Brassica juncea) against PARP1 and CK2α regulatory kinases using molecular docking and explicit-solvent molecular dynamics simulations.
